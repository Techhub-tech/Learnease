About LearnEase

LearnEase is a student-first learning tool that helps you understand and simplify complex notes, questions, and even code — in your own language.

Whether you're preparing for WAEC, JAMB, a university exam, or learning to code — LearnEase breaks everything down into clear, simple explanations in English, Hausa, or Arabic.

No account. No internet required. No cost. Just paste and learn.

🎯
Our Mission

Make quality education accessible to every student in Nigeria and beyond — regardless of language, device, or internet access.

How to Use LearnEase

Ready in under 60 seconds. No sign-up required.

1

Choose Your Mode

Select Notes / Questions for regular study notes, or Explain Code if you want to understand HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, or any programming code.

2

Paste Your Content

Copy and paste your lecture notes, textbook text, question, or code into the text box. You can also upload a .txt or code file.

3

Choose a Language

Pick English, Hausa, or Arabic — get your explanation in whichever language you understand best.

4

Tap Generate

Click the Generate button. LearnEase will produce a Summary and a plain-language Explanation — or a code walkthrough if you're in Code mode.

5

Listen, Save, or Share

Use Read Aloud to listen, Save to keep it in My Library (offline), or Share to send to classmates on WhatsApp or Telegram.

💡 Code Tip: Paste any HTML, CSS, JS, or Python and LearnEase explains every part in simple English!
📴 Offline Tip: LearnEase saves everything to your phone. No internet? No problem. Your library is always available.
🔗 Share Tip: Hit the Share button on any result to send it to WhatsApp or Telegram instantly.
