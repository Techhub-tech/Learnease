About LearnEase

LearnEase is a student-first learning tool that helps you understand and simplify complex notes, questions, and code — in your own language.

Whether you're preparing for WAEC, JAMB, a university exam, or learning to code — LearnEase breaks everything down into clear, simple explanations in English, Hausa, or Arabic.

No account. No internet required after first load. No cost. Just paste and learn.

🎯

How to Use LearnEase

Ready in under 60 seconds. No sign-up ever required.

1

Choose Your Mode

Select Notes/Questions for study notes, or Explain Code for HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, etc.

2

Paste Your Content

Copy and paste your notes, questions, or code. You can also upload a text or code file directly.

3

Choose a Language

Pick English, Hausa, or Arabic — whichever you understand best.

4

Tap Generate

Click Generate. You'll get a Summary, a Plain Explanation, and a Code Breakdown if you pasted code.

5

Listen, Save, or Share

Use Read Aloud, Save to offline library, or Share to WhatsApp/Telegram.

💡 Code: Paste any HTML, CSS, JS, or Python — LearnEase explains every part simply!
📴 Offline: Everything saves to your phone. No internet? No problem!
🔗 Share: The Share button works with WhatsApp, Telegram, and every other app on your phone.
